Acknowledgments

Elon Musk took a bet on me and many others. Working with and for him

pulled more out of me than I knew was there. I’m grateful for that

experience.

I’m also grateful to all the people who gave interviews and contributed

to the real world, real people examples of applying the Algorithm, including

and especially my partners now at DVx, who were the core strength of our

management team at Tesla: Karim Bousta, Ganesh Srivats, Nikki

Monterroso, and Michael Rossiter. Their fingerprints are all over the

examples and content of this book, and I get to wake up everyday and do

the work of my life alongside of them.

Fred Musone mentored me and taught me many of the principles that are

the bedrock of the Algorithm. Fred led thousands across dozens of factories,

then was lucky enough to learn these principles in Japan. He then taught me

and many others, giving us skills to see things in a way that many aren’t

able to. That’s an unfair advantage, and Fred planted that in me with patient

coaching and teaching. His mantra is “great leaders are also great teachers.”

Fred personifies that. And I want to be like Fred.

Stephen Baker, my coauthor of this book, helped me rediscover how

much joy I get out of writing. He patiently taught me his craft and his

discipline (pros always make the difficult look easy, and that is certainly

true of Steve). Jim Levine provided insights and edits, but most important,

the encouragement to write this. He wanted the world to read this book, and

that kept me at the keyboard on many days when I thought I didn’t have the

juice to keep writing.

The team at Penguin Random House, led by Adrian Zackheim, got

behind this project from the very beginning, bringing great talent on board,

including Anu Roy-Chaudhury as editor. Anu made Steve and I better each

step of the way with her challenges to do more for the reader. It was

Adrian’s conviction in this book that made our publishing partner decision

easy.

Ashley Langlais is more than my chief of staff; she is a brilliant and

skilled operator. Often, great talent doesn’t know how good they are, and

Ashley is certainly in that category. Ashley became a skilled book editor

during this project and organized our work so that I could do my day job(s)

and still get this book completed. She made Steve and me a much more

effective team.

My family is the inspiration of my life. They encouraged me to join

Tesla, and they were the first to help me see that it was time to leave. In

between, they cheered me on. Connor and Hannah, you are both flying on

your own now, and I am so proud of who you are as people. Both of you

combine talent and kindness in a way that makes the people around you

better. And to my dear wife, Heidi, who is the rock of our family. The single

best decision I made in life was leaping at the chance to marry you.

Everything since has flowed from that single decision.

Thank you all. I’m grateful.

# 致谢

Acknowledgments

埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）在我和许多人身上都下过赌注。与他共事、为他工作，把我身上自己都不知道的潜能激发了出来。我对那段经历心怀感激。

我同样感激所有接受采访、为本书"算法"在现实世界、真实人物身上的应用案例贡献力量的人，尤其是我如今在 DVx 的伙伴们——他们曾是特斯拉管理团队的中坚力量：卡里姆·布斯塔（Karim Bousta）、加内什·斯里瓦斯（Ganesh Srivats）、尼基·蒙特罗索（Nikki Monterroso）和迈克尔·罗西特（Michael Rossiter）。本书的诸多案例与内容都留下了他们的印记，而每天清晨醒来，我能和他们并肩，做我此生挚爱的事业。

弗雷德·穆索尼（Fred Musone）是我的导师，他传授给我许多构成算法根基的原则。弗雷德曾统领数十座工厂、数千名员工，之后有幸在日本习得了这些原则。随后他又教给我和许多人，赋予我们一种多数人并不具备的看世界的方式。这是一种不对等的优势，而弗雷德以耐心的辅导与教诲将它在我的心里播下种子。他的信条是"伟大的领导者也是伟大的老师"。弗雷德本人就是这句话的化身。而我想成为弗雷德那样的人。

本书的合著者斯蒂芬·贝克（Stephen Baker）帮我重新发现了写作带给我的那份喜悦。他耐心地把他的技艺与自律教给我——专业高手[1]总能让艰难之事显得轻而易举，史蒂夫正是如此。吉姆·莱文（Jim Levine）提供了真知灼见与编辑建议，但更重要的是，他鼓励我把这本书写下去。他希望全世界都能读到这本书，正是这份心意，让我在许多自以为再也写不动的日子里，仍守在键盘前。

企鹅兰登（Penguin Random House）的团队在阿德里安·扎克海姆（Adrian Zackheim）带领下，从一开始就全力支持这个项目，并引入了出众的人才，其中包括担任编辑的阿努·罗伊-乔杜里（Anu Roy-Chaudhury）。阿努不断鞭策我们为读者做得更多，让史蒂夫和我在每一步都变得更好。正是阿德里安对本书的笃信，让我们选择出版合作伙伴一事变得轻而易举。

阿什莉·朗格莱（Ashley Langlais）不只是我的幕僚长[2]；她是一位聪慧而干练的操盘手。才华横溢之人往往意识不到自己有多出色，而阿什莉无疑属于此类。在这个项目中，阿什莉成长为一名娴熟的图书编辑，并把我们的工作打理得井井有条，使我既能做好日常本职，又终究把这本书写完。她让史蒂夫和我成了一支高效得多的团队。

我的家人是我生命的灵感之源。他们鼓励我加入特斯拉，也是最先帮我看清"该离开的时候到了"的人。在这中间，他们一直为我呐喊助威。康纳（Connor）和汉娜（Hannah），你们俩如今都已独自展翅高飞，身为你们的父亲，我由衷为你们感到骄傲。你们二人将才华与善良融为一体，让身边的人也变得更好。还有我亲爱的妻子海蒂（Heidi），她是我们家庭的定海神针。我一生中做过最好的一个决定，就是抓住机会娶了你。此后的一切，都从这一个决定中流淌而出。

感谢你们所有人。我满怀感激。
